Research & Publications

arXiv Preprint · 2025

Global PIQA: Evaluating Physical Commonsense Reasoning Across 100+ Languages and Cultures (v0.1)

My first research paper is publicly available on arXiv.org. I contributed to this Multilingual Representation Learning (MRL) Workshop initiative by creating and submitting the Burushaski benchmark dataset — proudly representing one of the most unique low-resource languages of Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan.

This is a participatory commonsense reasoning benchmark covering over 100 languages and cultures — 116 language varieties across five continents, 14 language families, and 23 writing systems. It is a global collaboration involving 335 researchers from 65 countries. While state-of-the-art large language models (LLMs) perform well overall, they show up to a 37% accuracy gap in low-resource languages.

Acknowledgments: Sincere thanks to Adv. Wazir Shafi Lala, Quwáț K. Sunny Lala, and Jamsheed Aly Figaar for their invaluable support during the Burushaski dataset scripting and development process. Special thanks to lead authors Tyler A. Chang (University of California) and Catherine Arnett (EleutherAI) for their coordination, mentorship, and leadership throughout the Global PIQA initiative.
