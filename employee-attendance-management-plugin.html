About the Developer

I'm Sardar Ali Khamosh — a WordPress developer and HR tech builder. I created this plugin to solve a real problem at MedLink Analytics: tracking night-shift attendance with zero friction for employees and full visibility for management.

🏗️

The Problem I Solved

Managing night-shift attendance (7 PM–4 AM) manually was error-prone. Employees forgot to log hours, absences went untracked, and monthly HR reports took days. This plugin automates everything.

Smart Attendance Tracking

Employees visit /attendance, log in with email and password, and check in or out. Every session is timestamped, classified, and stored automatically.

  • Check-in status: On Time, Late, or Very Late based on grace period
  • Auto-checkout after 6 hours if employee forgets — labeled "Forgotten Checkout"
  • Auto-absence marked daily at 2 AM for any working day with no record
  • Working days: Monday to Friday only
  • Night shift default: 7:00 PM to 4:00 AM (fully configurable)
  • Grace period: 20 minutes before and after shift start

Workflow

Up and running in minutes.

From installation to your first checked-in employee in four simple steps.

1

Install Plugin

Upload ZIP via WordPress → Plugins → Add New. Activate and flush permalinks.

2

Configure Settings

Set office hours, grace period, salary period day, and working days from the Settings panel.

3

Add Employees

Create employee profiles with email, password, department, and position via the admin panel.

4

Share & Go Live

Share yoursite.com/attendance with employees. They log in and check in/out daily.
